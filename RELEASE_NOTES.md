**SCC Presenter — Beta** · free, open-source church presentation software. Created by AOG Sandton City Church.

The beta is open to the first 20 testers who join at **aogsccyouth.com/presenter** (until 31 December 2026). Please send feedback from the app (top bar → Feedback). Full release: 31 January 2027.

### Download
- **Windows 10/11:** `SCC-Presenter-Setup.exe` — double-click to install. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click **More info → Run anyway** (the app isn't code-signed yet).
- **Mac (Apple Silicon and Intel):** `SCC-Presenter.dmg` — open it and drag SCC Presenter into Applications. The first time, Control-click the app → **Open** (or System Settings → Privacy & Security → **Open Anyway**).

### What's in this preview
- Songs with automatic verse/chorus slides; import from FreeShow, OpenLP/OpenLyrics and text
- Bible passages (KJV, WEB, ASV, BBE, YLT), cached for offline use
- Pictures, videos and audio; backgrounds and church logo
- Output to a projector/TV, stage screen with clock, timer and messages
- Phone remote, OBS Studio / vMix browser source and transparent overlay
- Share services as files or over the local network
- AI helpers: tidy lyrics, verses for a topic, sermon notes to slides
- Automatic updates on Windows; update notices on Mac
- Setup panel: every connected screen (resolution, refresh rate, scale) and speaker/microphone
- Sound: pick the speaker, Clear voice / Warm music presets, built-in worship pads in any key
- Moving backgrounds, Easy mode, Light mode for older computers, first-run guide
